    FAQ

    Quick answers

    The questions we get most often. Still stuck? Send us a message above and we'll sort it out.

    How long does delivery take?

    Orders inside Cairo arrive in 2–4 business days. Other governorates usually take 4–7 business days. You'll get a tracking link as soon as your parcel leaves the shop.

    Can I return a book?

    Yes — you have 14 days from delivery. The book needs to be in its original condition, and we'll refund the full price once it's back on our shelf.

    Do you ship internationally?

    Not yet. We currently ship across Egypt only, but international shipping is on our roadmap. Drop us a line and we'll let you know when it's live.
