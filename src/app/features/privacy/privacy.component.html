01

What we collect

What you give us

Name, email, phone number, delivery address, and the messages you send us. If you create an account, we also store your password — hashed, never in plain text.

What we collect automatically

Pages you visit, how long you stay, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. This is aggregated and never tied to your name.

What you order

The books in your basket, order history, and delivery status — needed to fulfil your order and to handle returns or refunds.

What we never see

Your full card number or CVV. Payments are handled by a certified payment provider; we only receive a confirmation that the payment succeeded.

02

How we use it

  • To process orders, deliver books, and send you order updates.
  • To recommend titles you might enjoy — only if you've opted in. Unsubscribe in one click, any time.
  • To understand which pages work and fix the ones that don't.
  • To prevent fraud, secure accounts, and meet our legal obligations.

Our legal basis: we rely on the contract we have with you to fulfil orders, our legitimate interest in running a safe store, and your consent for marketing emails.

03

Cookies

Cookies are tiny files stored in your browser. We keep ours to a minimum — just enough to remember who you are and what's in your basket.

Cookie Purpose Duration
session_id Keeps you signed in while you browse. Session
cart Remembers the books in your basket. 30 days
preferences Stores your language and display choices. 1 year
analytics Anonymous counts of visits and page views. 13 months

You can clear or block cookies from your browser settings at any time. Blocking the essential ones will break sign-in and checkout.

04

Who we share it with

We share the minimum needed, only with people who help us deliver your order:

  • Couriers — your name, address, and phone number, so the parcel actually reaches you.
  • Payment providers — to process your payment securely.
  • Hosting & analytics — under contracts that forbid them from using your data for anything else.
  • Authorities — only when the law requires it.

We never sell your personal data. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.

06

Your rights

You stay in control of your information. You can ask us to:

  • Access a copy of what we hold.
  • Correct anything inaccurate.
  • Delete your account and data.
  • Export your data in a readable file.
  • Opt out of marketing at any time.
  • Complain to your data authority.

Email us at hello@bookhaven.com and we'll respond within 30 days. We may ask you to verify your identity first — that's for your protection.

07

Security

Passwords are hashed, traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, and access to customer data is limited to the few people who need it to do their job. We take regular backups and keep our systems patched.

No system is perfectly secure, though. If we ever discover a breach that affects you, we'll tell you promptly — and if you suspect your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

08

Children's privacy

BookHaven is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us their details, email us and we'll delete the data right away.

09

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes we'll also email registered customers before the new policy takes effect.

10

Contact us

Questions about this policy or the data we hold? Email hello@bookhaven.com or use the contact form. We aim to reply within one business day.
